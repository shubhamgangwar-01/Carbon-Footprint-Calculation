# Data sources (quick reference)

The full audit trail is in [DATASET_RESEARCH_AND_ACQUISITION_REPORT.md](DATASET_RESEARCH_AND_ACQUISITION_REPORT.md). The machine-readable registry is `data/metadata/source_registry.json`.

| Category | Primary source (used) | Version | Geography | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Grid electricity | Central Electricity Authority, *CO₂ Baseline Database for the Indian Power Sector* | v22.0, Aug 2026 (FY 2025-26) | India | GoI publication |
| Grid electricity (UK), fuels, flights, coach, cars, taxis, motorbikes, rail proxies, hotel stays, materials, waste, water | UK DESNZ *GHG Conversion Factors for Company Reporting* | 2026 v1.2 (2025 retained) | UK; hotel factors country-specific | OGL v3.0 |
| Road vehicles, city bus (India) | India GHG Program, *India Specific Road Transport Emission Factors* | v1.0, 2015 | India | © India GHG Program (cited) |
| Rail (India) | India GHG Program, *India Specific Rail Transport Emission Factors* | v1.0, 2015 (FY 2014-15) | India | © India GHG Program (cited) |
| Hotels | Cornell Hotel Sustainability Benchmarking Index 2021 (via DESNZ) | 2021 medians | 39 countries/cities | OGL (via DESNZ) |
| Food | Poore & Nemecek (2018), *Science* 360:987, via Our World in Data | 2010 data | Global mean | CC BY 4.0 |
| Tea | Premalatha et al. (2024), *Plant Science Today* 11(sp4), doi:10.14719/pst.5374 | 2024 | India (Tamil Nadu) | cited value |
| Meal composition | ICMR-NIN, *My Plate for the Day* policy brief | 2024 | India | GoI publication |
| Waste (reference only) | US EPA WARM | v16, Dec 2023 | USA | Public domain |
| Airport coordinates | OurAirports | snapshot | Global | Public domain |

**Not acquired, and not bypassed:**

* CHSB tool (registration required)
* ICE database (licence)
* EPD product declarations (product-specific)
* IEA emission factors (paid)
* ICAO calculator tables (not open)
* Poore & Nemecek supplementary data (publisher terms)
* Smart Freight Centre India v1.2 (HTTP 403; freight only)
